# Brand notes (from yalebulldogblue.com research, Problem 3)

Reference only. Site copy for Campus Customs is written in our own voice; don't copy text from the reference site.

- **Store:** "Yale Bulldog Blue by Campus Customs", an officially licensed Yale merch shop at 57 Broadway, New Haven, CT 06511.
- **Sells:** clothing (tees, sweatshirts, jackets, activewear, polos, bottoms; men's, women's, youth, infant), accessories (hats, bags, jewelry, pets, decals), and home goods (drinkware, pennants, books, stationery).
- **Collections:** the 14 residential colleges, ~20 varsity sports, graduate & professional schools, class years, alumni, and family members (Mom, Dad, Grandpa...).
- **Tone:** short, upbeat school-spirit lines built around Bulldog pride (e.g. "classic Bulldog pride", "the Bulldog way").
- **Policies:** custom items can't be returned or exchanged. Refund, terms and privacy policies exist but aren't summarized here, so the agent must not invent shipping or return details.
- **Visual style:** navy `#00366b` and white, cream footer `#f5f3ed`, near-black text `#1d1d1d`, Open Sans body, clean grid of product photos.
- **Our catalogue (DB)** is a subset: 102 apparel items (hoodies, crewnecks, quarter-zips, tees, jackets), priced $32–$98, in sizes XS–XXL.
